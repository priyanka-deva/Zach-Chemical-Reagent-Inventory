## Interpretation rules

A named platform relationship does not establish that a company uses its biological critical-reagent inventory module. Historical customer cases are dated. Supplier capabilities are described as supplier claims unless independently supported. Job postings identify advertised responsibilities, not verified system deployments. Merck products and pipeline are scientific context only; this assessment does not cover Merck's internal inventory or systems.

## Source discipline

Each substantive claim has an in-report reference identifier and direct source URL. Published dates, accessible-source verification, and the limits of each citation are listed in the source appendix. The report intentionally makes no claim of a dominant critical biological reagent inventory platform among the 19 peers.

The final formatted **Word** version is checked into the repository as **V1.0**. The full-text research companion is retained for quick text navigation.
