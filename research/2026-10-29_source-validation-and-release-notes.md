# Source validation and release notes — 29 October 2026

## Release scope
The manufacturing peer-inventory report begins with the requester’s primary question: what publicly documented peer biopharma systems and operating practices manage biological critical reagents? The Merck portfolio appears only as later scientific context; this is not an internal Merck technology assessment.
